# The System Dashboard

The Dashboard (`/cpms/cpmsDashBoard/indexNew`) serves as the administrative command center and primary landing page for the Real-Time Survey platform (RT-CPMS). 

![System Dashboard Preview](/images/dashboard_overview.png)

It is designed to give survey managers an immediate overview of active projects, quick links to essential tools, and a centralized hub to navigate across all major platform modules.

## Hauptfunktionen

### 1. Project & Form Selection
The left-hand panel contains the **Forms and Reports** navigator. This area lists all active surveys in your workspace. 
* By selecting a specific survey (e.g., *RTA - SURVEY 02*), you direct the dashboard to focus monitoring and metrics solely on that project.

### 2. Visualization & Metric Filters
Above the project list, you can toggle between several critical data perspectives to monitor real-time fieldwork progress:
* **Count by start time / end time**: Track when enumerators are beginning and completing their survey sessions.
* **Count by submission date**: Monitor the overall daily volume of data reaching the server.
* **Count by username**: Evaluate individual enumerator productivity and performance.
* **Map of interviews**: View a geographic (GIS) distribution of where survey responses are being collected to ensure spatial coverage requirements are met.

### 3. Application Portals
The center of the dashboard provides instant access to the data collection interfaces. Depending on your enumerators' hardware, you can launch or direct them to:
* **Web App**: For browser-based data collection.
* **Android App**: Link to the Google Play Store or APK.
* **iOS App**: Link to the Apple App Store.

### 4. Direkte Modul-Verknüpfungen
Three prominent action buttons allow rapid transition to the most frequently used operational modules:
* **Form & Data Entry**: Jump directly to manually managing collected data.
* **Analytics & Reports**: Open the Business Intelligence (BI) suite to cross-tabulate and chart survey answers.
* **Permission Configuration**: Adjust who has access to the active survey and what roles they hold.

### 5. Globale Navigationsseitenleiste
The collapsible left sidebar provides access to the complete ecosystem of RT-CPMS backend modules. From here, you can dive deep into:
* **Setting Up**: Managing staff and active devices.
* **Fieldwork Management**: Tracking enumerators' daily activity.
* **Quality Assurance**: Implementing and reviewing QA rules and flags.
* **Final Deliverables**: Exporting your cleaned datasets to CSV, PDF, or Stata.
