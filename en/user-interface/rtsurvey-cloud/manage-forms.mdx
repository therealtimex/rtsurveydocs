The **Manage Forms** module serves as the primary gateway for organizing your survey instruments and coordinating fieldwork tasks. It provides fine-grained control over form accessibility, data viewing, and device targeting.

![Form Management Interface](/images/manage_forms.png)

## Form Family Overview

Under the **Form & Data Entry** menu, the **Form Family** section displays a comprehensive grid of all your imported forms along with essential quick-access controls. This centralized view allows project managers to quickly deploy and configure individual surveys.

### Key Data Columns & Configuration Options

- **Data View:** Access submitted records and dataset previews directly for each form.
- **Allow Edit:** A quick toggle to lock or unlock the ability for field workers to edit their submitted data (Active/Inactive statuses).
- **Public Visibility:** A toggle to make a form publicly available without requiring enumerator authentication.
- **Webform's URL:** Instantly retrieve the unique public link to share browser-based data collection forms. 
- **Override Settings:** An advanced configurations modal (accessed via the cog icon) for tailoring form-specific rules, values, and disabling specific features.
- **Form Identifiers:** View standard metadata, including the Form Family name, Family Code, and a unique Form ID.
- **Geo Configuration:** Specific mapping or geo-point configurations tied to the form structure.
- **Lock Form:** Feature to lock submission access to finalizing the data collection phase.

## Form Actions and Navigation

The top control panel facilitates batch operations and alternative form views:

- **Action to Device:** A powerful drop-down tool enabling project managers to assign tasks, send targeted forms, or dispatch actions directly to specific enumerators' field devices.
- **XLSX Form Tab:** Quickly navigate from the main management grid to upload environments for handling new native XLSForm files.
- **Form Builder Tab:** Access the built-in tool for visually constructing or modifying forms dynamically without using exterior spreadsheet software.
