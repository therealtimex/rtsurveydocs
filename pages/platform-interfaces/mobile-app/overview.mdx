The rtSurvey mobile app provides survey and analytics services for business clients and development partners. Its outstanding advantage is real-time technology in data collection and analytics that seamlessly works on both Cloud and Mobile devices.

## Key Features

- **Real-time Data Collection**: Capture data in the field and sync it instantly with the cloud.
- **Seamless Integration**: Works in harmony with the rtSurvey Cloud for project management and analysis.
- **Multi-platform Support**: Available for both Android and iOS devices.
- **Offline Capability**: Collect data even without an active internet connection and sync later.

Whether you're conducting market research, field assessments, or complex academic studies, the rtSurvey mobile app provides the tools needed for efficient and accurate data collection.
