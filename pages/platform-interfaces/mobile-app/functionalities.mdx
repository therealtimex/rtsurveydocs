## Fill Form

Access blank survey forms assigned to you. Forms are downloaded automatically — no manual retrieval needed. You can fill forms offline and sync when connectivity is restored.

## Edit Form

Manage survey instances across three tabs:

- **Saved** — Resume incomplete forms saved on your device.
- **Returned** — Edit forms that were previously submitted and sent back for correction.
- **Forwarded** — Access forms that originated on another device and were forwarded to you.

## Send Form

Submit completed forms to the server. Data is transmitted in real time, making it immediately available for review and analysis.

## Notifications

View notices and updates pushed from the server, such as new assignments, submission confirmations, or project announcements.

## Documents

Access documents shared by administrators or project managers — reference materials, instructions, or attachments related to your survey project.

## Reports

View analytical reports sent from the server directly on your device.

## Review Form

Inspect finalized survey instances across four tabs:

- **Finalized** — Completed forms not yet submitted.
- **Sent** — Forms successfully submitted to the server.
- **Transferred** — Forms moved to another device.
- **Received** — Forms originally created on another device and transferred to yours.

All instances are view-only by default. Edit access can be requested and approved by an administrator or project manager.

## Map

View a map showing the GPS location recorded for each survey submission. Useful for verifying field coverage and spotting geographic patterns.

## Exit

Close the app while staying signed in. Reopening the app resumes your session without requiring you to log in again.
