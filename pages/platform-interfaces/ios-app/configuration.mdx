After connecting to a server, you may want to configure the rtSurvey mobile app to suit your specific needs and ensure optimal performance.

## Settings Overview

The app settings allow you to manage:

- **Data Syncing**: Choose how and when your data is synced with the cloud.
- **User Interface**: Adjust display settings for better visibility in the field.
- **GPS Accuracy**: Configure location tracking settings for more precise data.
- **Offline Storage**: Manage how much data is stored locally on your device.
